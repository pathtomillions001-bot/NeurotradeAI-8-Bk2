# Recovery Quality Pipeline v2 — Mathematical & System Design

## 1. Overview & Non-Negotiable Invariants

Recovery Quality Pipeline v2 (`artifacts/api-server/src/lib/autonomous-hedge/`) replaces the ad-hoc additive recovery candidate ranking (`S_0 + 18w(P - 0.5) - 18w R_{3L} - 8\sqrt{\text{Var}}`, rematch penalty, and 2–3 tick confirmation delay) with a single **Episode-Distribution-Aware Soft Cost $J(c)$** fed by a **Multi-Horizon Universal-Threshold Shrinkage Estimator $\tilde{p}$** and a **Bounded Value-of-Waiting Controller**.

All non-negotiable invariants are preserved:
- `recovery-math.ts`, `agents/recovery-engine.ts`, and `recovery-payout.ts` are untouched.
- Every recovery stake is still computed by `getDynamicRecoveryStake(...)` in `cycle.ts`.
- `mode === "NORMAL"` is byte-for-byte unchanged (`useV2Recovery = mode === "RECOVERY" && v2Mode !== "off"`).
- No hard eligibility gate can stall recovery indefinitely: waiting is strictly bounded by at most **3 fresh ticks or 6 seconds wall-clock, whichever comes first**.
- Feature flag `RECOVERY_V2_MODE: "off" | "shadow" | "on"` in `constants.ts` defaults to `"shadow"`.

## 2. Core Equations

### 2.1 M1 — Multi-Horizon Universal-Threshold Evidence (`evidence.ts`)

For each market $m \in \{1, \dots, M\}$ ($M = 19$) and each contract specification $s$ in the active recovery set ($S$ specs), the known fair win probability under the null is:

$$p_0(s) = \begin{cases}
(9 - d)/10 & \text{Over } d \\
d/10 & \text{Under } d \\
0.50 & \text{Even, Odd, Rise, Fall} \\
0.10 & \text{Matches} \\
0.90 & \text{Differs}
\end{cases}$$

Across windows $w \in \mathcal{W} = \{120, 500, 1500\}$ (clamped to available tape length up to 1,500 ticks):
- **Out-of-sample split for auto-digit specs (`DIGITMATCH`, `DIGITDIFF` with `barrier < 0`):** the older $70\%$ of window $w$ selects the candidate digit $d^*$, and only the newest $30\%$ ($n_{\text{eff}} = \lceil 0.30 n_w \rceil$) evaluates $z_w$.
- **Standardised $z$-score:**
  $$z_w = \frac{\text{hits}_w - n_{\text{eff}} \, p_0}{\sqrt{n_{\text{eff}} \, p_0 (1 - p_0)}}$$
- **Universal soft-thresholding across the search pool:**
  $$M_{\text{eff}} = M \cdot S \cdot |\mathcal{W}| \times \begin{cases} 10 & \text{if auto-digit} \\ 1 & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}, \qquad \lambda = \sqrt{2 \ln M_{\text{eff}}}$$
  $$\tilde{z}_w = \operatorname{sign}(z_w) \max(0, |z_w| - \lambda), \qquad \tilde{p}_w = p_0 + \tilde{z}_w \sqrt{\frac{p_0(1 - p_0)}{n_{\text{eff}}}}$$
  The window $w^*$ maximising $|\tilde{z}_w|$ is selected (ties broken in favour of the longest window).
- **First-order Markov dependence check:** over the longest window, let $(LL, LW, WL, WW)$ be the $2 \times 2$ transition counts, $n_L = LL + LW$, $n_W = WL + WW$, $a = P(\text{win} \mid \text{loss}) = LW / n_L$, and $b = P(\text{win} \mid \text{win}) = WW / n_W$. With variance-floored standard error $\text{se}_{\text{dep}} = \sqrt{\frac{\operatorname{Var}_L}{n_L} + \frac{\operatorname{Var}_W}{n_W}}$:
  $$z_{\text{dep}} = \frac{a - b}{\text{se}_{\text{dep}}}, \qquad \tilde{z}_{\text{dep}} = \operatorname{sign}(z_{\text{dep}}) \max(0, |z_{\text{dep}}| - \lambda)$$
  When $\tilde{z}_{\text{dep}} \neq 0$, the shrunk conditional shift $\widetilde{\Delta}_{ab} = \tilde{z}_{\text{dep}} \cdot \text{se}_{\text{dep}}$ adjusts $\tilde{p}$ according to the current last outcome:
  $$\tilde{p} \leftarrow \tilde{p}_{w^*} + \begin{cases} -\frac{n_L}{n_L + n_W}\widetilde{\Delta}_{ab} & \text{if last tick won} \\ +\frac{n_W}{n_L + n_W}\widetilde{\Delta}_{ab} & \text{if last tick lost} \end{cases}$$
- **Empirical-Bayes market-wide `edgeIndex`:** across all $K$ longest-window $z$-scores in the current tick, $\hat{\tau}^2 = \max\!\left(0, \frac{1}{K}\sum_{k=1}^K z_k^2 - 1\right)$, smoothed by an EWMA with half-life $600$ ticks.

### 2.2 M2 — Recovery Episode Simulator & Cache (`ladder-outcomes.ts`)

Given $(\tilde{p}, \text{payout})$ and the current recovery state $(\text{method}, \text{autoMode}, \text{multiplier}, \text{step}, \text{debt}, \text{targetRemaining}, \text{base}, \text{maxTradeStake}, \text{balance}, n_{\text{Stop}})$ where $n_{\text{Stop}} = \max(1, \text{consecutiveLossLimit} - \text{currentLossRun})$, we simulate $N = 2000$ paths of horizon $H = 40$ steps using:
- Precomputed Common Random Numbers $U_{i, h} \in [0, 1)$ generated by `mulberry32` indexed by `(pathIdx, stepIdx)`.
- The exact `calculateRecoveryStakeRequest`, `applyRecoveryStakeLimits`, and `settleRecoveryWin` functions from `recovery-math.ts`.
- Rao-Blackwellized expected episode P&L via Wald's identity:
  $$\text{meanPnl} = (\tilde{p} \cdot \text{payout} - 1) \cdot \frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^N \text{totalStake}_i$$
- $\text{es5}$: mean P&L of the worst $5\%$ ($100$) episode paths.
- $p_{\text{Stop}}$: fraction of paths experiencing $\ge n_{\text{Stop}}$ consecutive losses inside the episode.
- $p_{\text{CapHit}}$: fraction of paths where a requested or applied stake reaches $\min(\text{maxTradeStake}, \text{balance})$.

**Cache key format:**
```text
${method}:${autoMode}:${mult2dp}:${step}:${maxSteps}:${debtCents}:${targetCents}:${baseCents}:${capCents}:${balCents}:${pBucket0.005}:${payout2dp}:${nStop}:${paths}:${horizon}:${seed}
```
`simulateEpisodeStatsCached` linearly interpolates between adjacent $0.005$ $p$-buckets and invalidates on settlement or settings change.

### 2.3 M3 — Single Soft Cost $J(c)$ (`score-v2.ts`)

Every candidate $c$ is scored by its episode cost in units of base stakes (lower $J$ is better; `score = -J`):

$$J(c) = -\frac{\text{meanPnl}(c)}{\text{base}} + \lambda_{\text{tail}} \frac{\max(0, -\text{es5}(c))}{\text{base}} + \lambda_{\text{stop}} \, p_{\text{Stop}}(c)$$

with $\lambda_{\text{tail}} = 0.25$ and $\lambda_{\text{stop}} = 4.0$.
- Evidence enters **only** through $\tilde{p}$ inside `simulateEpisodeStatsCached`.
- Ties within $|\Delta J| < 0.001$ (with equal $\tilde{z}$) are broken by a deterministic hash of `tickSequence` and candidate key so equal-cost markets rotate evenly without concentration.

### 2.4 M4 — Bounded Value-of-Waiting (`timing-v2.ts`)

Tracks a rolling 500-evaluation window of 1–3-tick improvements in baseline-normalised $J_{\text{best}}$:

$$\text{valueOfWaiting} = \max\!\left(0, \; \overline{\Delta J}_{1..3}\right)$$

- **Immediate fire:** if $\text{valueOfWaiting} \le 0.02$, fire immediately (`waitTicks = 0`).
- **Bounded patience:** otherwise wait up to **at most 3 fresh ticks or 6 seconds wall-clock, whichever comes first** (never extended), firing early as soon as current $J_{\text{best}}$ is within $0.005$ of the best $J$ seen during the wait.
- **Pre-buy freshness check:** if $> 1$ tick elapsed since decision formation, `executeDecision` recomputes $J$ on the newest tape once and switches candidate at most once if beaten by $> 0.02$.

### 2.5 M5–M7 — Live Quote Prefetch, Shadow Ledger & CUSUM Calibration Guard

- **M5 (`quote-prefetch.ts`):** Refreshes live payouts via `resolveRecoveryPayout` every 25s while `lossRun >= 1`; flags reason text when live payout differs from table by $> 3\%$.
- **M6 (`shadow-ledger.ts`):** Every fresh tick, settles 1-tick outcomes for the previous tick's top-10 v2, top-10 v1, and 1 random control per family into a 20,000-record ring buffer + persisted aggregate counters (OLS calibration slope $\beta \pm \text{SE}$, $\kappa = \Phi(\beta / \text{SE})$, Brier skill vs $p_0$, and `edgeReport()`).
- **M7 (`calibration-guard.ts`):** One-sided CUSUM on real recovery trades $r_t = \text{win}_t - \tilde{p}_t$, $S_t = \max(0, S_{t-1} - r_t)$ with threshold $h = 4\sqrt{\tilde{p}(1-\tilde{p})}$. On alarm, sets `evidenceWeight = 0` ($\tilde{p} := p_0$) for 500 ticks.
